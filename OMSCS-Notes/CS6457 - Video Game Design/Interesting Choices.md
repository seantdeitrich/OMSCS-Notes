**Decision Types:**
- Hollow vs. Weighted Decisions
- Obvious Decisions
- Uninformed vs. Informed Decisions
- Dramatic Decisions (Emotional State)
- Immediate vs. Long Term Decisions

**Dilemmas** are situations where players must weigh the consequences of their choices carefully

**Zero Sum Game:**:
- Cutter vs Chooser example (Cake Cutting)
- Total amount won at the end of the game is exactly equal to the amount lost
- Each player maximizes their **minimum** potential result, as opposed to the maximum
- This has an obvious decision, and our goal is create more complex dilemmas
**Prisoner's Dilemma:**
- Two criminals in separate cells are given the following situation:
	- Rat on your partner and he denies it, you go free and your partner gets 5 years in jail
	- If both rat, then each gets 3 years
	- If neither rats, each gets 1 year
- In this case there is a 
	- Temptation for defection
	- Reward for mutual cooperation
	- Punishment for mutual defection
	- 'Suckers Payoff' for unreciprocated cooperation

**Puzzles:**
- Puzzles contextualize choice that the player makes
- There is innate tension in solving puzzles

**Rewards vs. Punishment**
- Games should generally focus on the threat of punishment, which builds tension
- Rewards should have some sort of utility or value
- Reward system that utilize operant conditioning are particularly addictive
