## 1. Quantitative Feedback (Likert Scale)
*Please rate your agreement (1: Strongly Disagree to 5: Strongly Agree):* 
* **Control Intuition**: I found the controls and general character movement to be intuitive. 
* **Mechanics**: The "Recall" mechanic felt responsive and easy to trigger.
* **Momentum**: I understood how to keep my momentum while catching the package. 
* **Enemy Interaction**: I felt throwing the package at an enemy was an effective way to defeat enemies.
* **Enemy Threat**: The enemies felt sufficiently threatening and forced me to change my pathing. 
* **Visual Clarity**: I felt that the animations of the character matched his actions. 
* **Level Design**: I enjoyed the overall design of the levels. 
* **Incentives**: I felt the need to grab all collectables (stamps) on the level.
* **Replayability**: Seeing the final delivery stats made me want to retry each level. 
* **Narrative Engagement**: I felt immersed in the dialogue and was motivated to read it. 
* **General Difficulty**: The overall difficulty of the levels felt appropriate and fair.
