# Deliverable 1: PlaytestScript.pdf

## 1. Setup & Preparation
* **Version**: Use the latest stable version of the project.
* **Anonymization**: Do not record play tester names, emails, or other personally identifying information. 
* **Recording**: Open a document for "Think-Aloud" notes to capture real-time feedback.
* **Timer**: Prepare to record the time taken for each level completion and the total playtest duration.

## 2. Scripted Introduction (Spoken to Playtester)
"Hi! Thank you for helping us test **DeadEx: Dungeon Explorer**. We are testing the game's design and mechanics, not your personal skill. 

Please do not feel pressured to give us high 'grades' or positive feedback just to please us; we need your honest perspective to improve the game. 

Please use the **Think-Aloud method**: narrate what you are trying to do, what you expect to happen, and any moments where you feel confused or excited. There are no wrong answers; your feedback helps us identify areas that need further refinement." 

## 3. Administration Steps
* **Initial Discovery**: Allow the player to start the game without providing any instructions regarding controls.
* **Assistance Protocol**: Do not explain controls unless the player explicitly asks for assistance or is unable to progress.
* **Level Progression**: Once the player completes the first level, the administrator may manually move them to the second level to continue the test.
* **Observation**: The administrator must take detailed notes on the player's "Think-Aloud" commentary, specifically focusing on how they learn new skills.
* **Data Collection**: Record the amount of time it takes for the player to complete specific actions and levels rather than enforcing strict time limits.
    - Leaving the first room (the ghost room)
    - Crossing the gap
    - Completing Level 1
    - ** Reset the Timer **
    - Starting the Maze
    - Completing Level 2

## 4. In-Software Game Event Logging
* **Efficiency**: Record the specific time taken to complete each individual level.
* **Engagement**: Note how many optional stamps were collected versus ignored.

## 5. Post-Session Debrief
"The playtest is now complete. Please fill out the following questionnaire. Your data will be used to create action items for our next development phase."
